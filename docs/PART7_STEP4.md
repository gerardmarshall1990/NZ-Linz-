# Part 7 step 4: building consent pack, statements, engineer reviews

## What exists
- **Pack builder** (`backend/app/consent_pack.py`, config `config/building_consent_checklist.yaml`): from a layout option it creates a versioned pack (a new version supersedes the old). Documents follow the Council folders (Applications, Plans, Supporting Documents) and file names.
- **Generated, all stamped DRAFT** (text and every PDF page): index, application data sheet (field by field, to type into the portal), lodgement checklist, owner and agent details, project description, change of use (existing vs proposed), specification schedule, Building Code compliance schedule (clause by clause), drawing set (PDF and DXF per scaled level).
- **Upload slots only** (nothing generated, no route can write one): record of title, structural calcs, fire report, PS1 design / structural / fire / services, PS2, certificate of work, technical literature.
- **Statements and the author register**: an upload must be a PDF, with author, company, date issued. It counts only if the author is on Auckland Council's producer statement author register: either matched in a register export you drop in `docs/sources_manual/ps_register/*.csv` (columns `name, company, registration_no, valid_until`) or recorded by you after the Council's online search (who checked, when). A check older than 30 days, a future date, a wrong type or "not on register" makes it invalid.
- **Completeness and the gate**: `POST /api/packs/{id}/ready-to-lodge` is refused with the list of blockers while: the checklist is not Council-verified, a required slot is empty or its statement is invalid, any `[TO COMPLETE]` remains in a required generated document, a level has no scale (no drawing), or a screening FAIL has no recorded resolution. Any later change returns the pack to draft.
- **Engineer reviews**: package per role (designer, fire, structural, services) with brief, wanted documents and drawings; download as zip, or (flag `engineer_review_links`, default OFF) a private 14-day link that records views, comments and return. Turnaround per role is measured (`/api/reviews/metrics`). The app sends nothing: you pass the link on. Engineer comments are comments, not approvals.
- **Feature flags** (`core.user_flag`, `/api/flags`): everything defaults off.

## Reconciled with the Council's AC1009 (2026-10-18)
The owner supplied AC1009 v.21 (June 2026). The pack now: asks every one of its 151 lines (Yes / N/A, or a whole section N/A) and refuses "ready to lodge" while any is unanswered; puts the AC1009 address, legal description, drawing number and title, designer, owner and date on every drawing; applies the 90-day rule to producer statements and the record of title; follows the fire-design route table (the fire engineer picks C/AS2, C/VM2 or alternative solution, which decides whether PS1, the fire engineering brief or the PS2 review evidence is required: my reading of page 4, for the fire engineer to confirm); adds slots for the existing-buildings assessment (s112/s115, Building Score Sheet), the accessibility report and the owner's letter of authorisation (required when an agent lodges). The Council's own form is attached by you; the app gives the answers sheet to copy from.

## Honest limits
- Auckland Council's checklist and forms (AC1021, AC2205, AC1826) could not be opened (the site refuses scripted requests and was not bypassed). The pack is built from the spec and from search-result summaries recorded in `docs/council_forms/README.md`. The portal field order is **not verified**. Fee schedule not found (placeholder in the pack).
- Which documents are required for a care-home change of use is my assumption until the Council checklist is reconciled.
- Statements are not carried over to a new pack version, deliberately: a changed design needs fresh statements.
- Public routes: only `/review/{token}` and `/api/review/{token}`, 404 while the flag is off. The app is on a private network (Tailscale), so engineers cannot reach a link unless you also give them access; the zip route works without that.
