# Auckland Council building consent: forms, checklist and lodgement rules (discovery record)

**Update 2026-10-18: AC1009 v.21 (June 2026) "Lodgement Checklist: Commercial" was supplied by the owner and read in full (all 19 pages).** It is the Council's own checklist: 45 sections and 151 Yes/N/A lines, now loaded in `config/ac1009_lodgement_checklist.yaml` and built into the pack (answers per line, section N/A, producer-statement lines flagged). Rules taken from it: drawings per AS/NZS 1100 with address, legal description, drawing number and title, designer, owner and date on every drawing; producer statements and the record of title no older than 90 days; a fire report unless the work has no fire cells; the fire-design route table (C/AS2, C/VM2, alternative solution) that decides which fire documents are needed; a deposit at lodgement; Watercare confirmation where a connection is available. The file is kept in `docs/sources_manual/council_forms/` (git-ignored). **Still not seen:** AC1021 (application pack 2), the online portal screens and field order, the fee schedule. The older table below is search-summary level.

**Status of discovery (last_verified 2026-10-18): URLs found, documents NOT opened.** Auckland Council's website answers scripted requests from this sandbox with `406 Not Acceptable` (its CDN filters non-browser clients) and the fetch tool cannot resolve the host, so none of the PDFs below could be downloaded. I did not try to get around the filter. What is recorded here comes from web-search result summaries and is therefore **unverified against the Council documents themselves**. Per MASTER_SPEC 8.1 the pack is built to a configurable checklist (`config/building_consent_checklist.yaml`) and **"ready to lodge" stays blocked until you supply the Council's current documents** and the checklist is reconciled with them (see `docs/sources_manual/README.md`).
