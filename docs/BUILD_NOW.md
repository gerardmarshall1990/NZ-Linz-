# What is being built now (one page, plain English)

**The main product: the Remote Conversion Pipeline (Part 7).** Pick a property (a listing, motel, lodge or house). The system finds or captures its existing plans (Council file, owner, or a phone scan done remotely), reads them into a structured model, proposes up to three rest home layouts as draft demolition/new-work drawings (stamped **DRAFT: NOT FOR CONSENT until reviewed and issued by a qualified professional**), works out what must change and what it costs, drafts the building consent pack, sends it to your review engineers, checks it against Council's checklist, and prepares lodgement. You approve every send and every lodgement with one tap. Site visits are the last resort.

## Build order
1. **Rest home finder.** Rental listings on a map with a rest home verdict (green, amber, red, grey), a feasibility for each property (beds, retrofit cost, profit, walk-away rent, break-even occupancy), and the **Offer Builder**: fixed rent, JV profit share, or hybrid with a minimum payment. It always shows the cash required and the downside cases, not just the best case. Needs: listings (Trade Me only through its official API if the terms allow, otherwise CSV; realestate.co.nz is blocked until a lawful route exists), the Part 7 pipeline, and your cost inputs.
2. **Development site finder.** Click any parcel: zone rules, how many lots or units fit, feasibility and profit, with every rule cited. Needs the rules corpus (done, awaiting your spot-check), slope for candidate parcels, and the layout and financial engines.
3. **Māori land finder.** Māori land blocks, the entity to approach (lease or JV, never "for sale"), zone rules for the land, and a draft proposal outline.

## One rule that changed
For rest home conversions of **existing** buildings, flood and other natural hazard flags are **information only**. They never change the verdict or the ranking. They only count for development-site feasibility (and for a new-build rest home).

## Everything else in MASTER_SPEC.md is LATER (not built unless you ask)
- Chat agent over the data (Stage 5)
- Whole-of-Auckland bulk screening of every dwelling
- Services module (Watercare, Vector, Healthy Waters distances)

- Scale layers (Part 8): Approval Queue, capture portal, playbooks, batch engineer workflow, capacity planner, dashboard. These queue up right after Part 7 steps 1 to 4, but only when you say go.
- **Best Use tab (Part 9)**: compares (a) converting the existing building, (b) developing or subdividing, (c) a new-build rest home, side by side with profit, cash, timeline, risks and confidence. It comes after items 1 and 2 above.
- Validation report, feeds admin page extras, documentation extras

## Always true
Real data or a clear block. Every number has a source and date. No outcome claims. Council decides every application. Nothing is generated that pretends to be a signature, producer statement, registration number or final fire report.
