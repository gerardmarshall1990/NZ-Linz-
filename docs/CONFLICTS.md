# Conflicts found in the spec and how each was resolved (MASTER_SPEC Part 5 section 1: the later Part wins; Part 0.4 decisions override everything)

| # | Conflict | Resolution | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The spec names the bulk screening table `core.rh_screen`, but the backup rule says every `core.rh_*` table is user data and goes in the small user-data backup. 362,000 regenerable rows do not belong there. | Table is `analytics.rh_screen`, rebuilt by `make screen`. | DECISIONS.md, db/sql/98_screen.sql |
| 2 | Part 2 section 0 forbids the word "approved" in any UI text, but Part 8 asks for an Approval Queue and approval records. | The feature is called Approvals; the stored status is `granted` (never "approved"); the forbidden-words test scans the code. | backend/app/approvals.py |
| 3 | Part 2 requires the "Rules-checked" badge when there are zero FAIL and zero UNKNOWN, but the vehicle access standards are not in the rules corpus. | Access is UNKNOWN in every development scenario, so the badge is never shown until those standards are loaded. | backend/app/devrules.py |
| 4 | Part 4 asks for conservative / base / max bed scenarios per dwelling; no source exists for the factors. | Only a base estimate is stored (cautious placeholder 60 m2 per bed, flagged); listing, licensed and plan data upgrade the tier. No invented factors. | config/screening.yaml |
| 5 | Part 4 lists overlay and hazard flags as screening columns; decision 13 says hazards never change a conversion verdict or ranking. | Hazard flags are stored and shown as information only for conversions; they affect development and new-build options and the Best Use "best" label. | screen.py, bestuse.py |
| 6 | Part 3 section 8 says to build the consent pack to "AC1021"; the checklist the Council actually requires with an application is AC1009 (supplied by the owner). | The pack is built to AC1009 v.21 (151 lines); AC1021, the portal screens and fees remain unseen; `council_verified` stays false until the owner says it has been checked. | docs/council_forms/README.md |
| 7 | BUILD_NOW.md put the development-site flow after the rest home finder and "only when you say go"; the owner's autopilot instruction later listed it. | The autopilot list (later instruction) wins; built after the rest home work. | docs/DECISIONS.md |
| 8 | Part 4 says field capture writes DOCUMENTED then CONFIRMED "after I review"; Part 8 says nothing is treated as confirmed without a human. | Field and portal captures are DOCUMENTED until the owner reviews them in the app. | backend/app/field.py |
| 9 | Part 2 asks for a services module using Watercare data; Part 0.9 forbids loading Watercare data without written permission. | Watercare data is not loaded; manual fields and links only; capacity is always UNKNOWN without an uploaded letter. | backend/app/services.py |
| 10 | The spec asks for routing with OSRM or pgRouting and forbids paid APIs without asking. | No routing engine is installed; call sheets use straight-line distances and say so. | backend/app/routeplan.py |
| 11 | Part 1 originally said listings are not available / CSV only; Part 4 section 2 allows Trade Me's official API if terms permit. | Trade Me connector built, off until terms are read and keys set; CSV is the live route. realestate.co.nz stays blocked. | backend/app/trademe.py |

## Roster coverage multiplier: 1.4 (2026-10-17) vs 1.0 (2026-10-19)
On 2026-10-17 the owner confirmed a roster cover multiplier of 1.4 and 4 positions per 8 beds. On 2026-10-19 the owner gave their own sums for a 6-bed home (2 staff in total at $800 each, about $1,000 a week of everything else) and I saved cover multiplier 1.0, 1 staff per 3 beds, with the cook, manager, clinical lead, utilities, insurance, repairs and audit lines at 0 (counted inside the misc figure). The later instruction is in force; the owner should confirm with an operator, as in BLOCKERS item 13. These are saved in the database as owner-entered, so they show on My inputs and can be changed there.
