# Bulk screening (Part 4) and the outreach layer

**Screening** (`make screen`, background `make screen-bg`, about 2 minutes for all of Auckland): `analytics.rh_screen`, one row per candidate dwelling (a building, in a zone where some residential-care activity has a consent path, not a school/hospital). Beds: confirmed plan facts > listing bedrooms (DOCUMENTED) > footprint estimate (ESTIMATED, with a cautious **placeholder** of 60 m2 per bed and 1 storey; `config/screening.yaml`). Economics use your inputs; blanks give no number. Incremental: only rows whose inputs or your assumptions changed are rewritten. The Screen tab and the Layers control (candidates) show it; filters, polygon, radius, shortlists with a re-run diff and CSV/XLSX export; suburb analytics (certified beds and beds per 1,000 aged 75+ are blank: HealthCERT list and census not loaded).
**House-scale and portfolio** (`/api/portfolio`): shared manager and clinical lead ratio, certification cost per site (yours; how multiple homes are certified is a NEEDS_PROFESSIONAL item), roll-out under your cash and staffing limits.
**Outreach layer, all OFF by default** (config/feature_flags.yaml, 403 until you edit the file after the validation report): field capture PWA (`/field`: permission first, DOCUMENTED until you review), CRM (`/crm`: source + lawful basis, do-not-contact, no tenants, audit log), call sheets and route plans (straight-line distances only: no routing engine installed), offer letters / emails / one-pagers / terms sheets (rent never above the walk-away rent, lawyer-review banner, unsubscribe line, approval record before "sent"). The app sends nothing.
